Privacy Policy.

This policy explains what information BRIDGEit collects, how we use it, when we share it, and the choices you have. It is written for the BRIDGEit website, its email one-time-code sign-in, and the community experiences available through the site.

Effective date: 9 October 2026 · Last reviewed: 9 October 2026

1. Scope and who this notice is for

This Privacy Policy applies to the BRIDGEit website at includemesurulere.world, the pages and features that link to this policy, and communications sent about those features. BRIDGEit is an inclusion-focused space for sign discovery, community, connection, learning, and opportunity. Some features are presented as a pilot or preview and may change as the service is developed.

In this policy, “BRIDGEit”, “we”, “us”, and “our” refer to the BRIDGEit project and the people who operate the website. “You” means a visitor, account holder, community contributor, trainer, or other person using the site. This notice describes the current product implementation; it does not replace any rights or duties that apply under mandatory privacy or consumer-protection law.

Our design promise: BRIDGEit does not ask you to provide a diagnosis or proof of disability to use the service. Please share only the personal information you are comfortable putting into a profile, post, message, pledge, video, or other contribution.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account and sign-in information

When you request an email one-time code, we receive your email address and create or use an account identifier through our authentication provider. We also receive the technical account data needed to keep a session active, such as a user ID, session timestamps, and confirmation state. The one-time code is used to verify the sign-in request; it is not a password that you choose or that we display to other people.

2.2 Profile and preference information

If you complete a profile, the site may store your name, first and last name, phone number, location, short biography, communication preference, sign-language proficiency, profile visibility choice, and related account settings. You decide which details to enter and which visibility option to select. A profile field may reveal information about accessibility, communication, or community participation. Treat anything you place in a public or member-visible field as information you have chosen to share with that audience.

2.3 Community content and interactions

Depending on the features you use, we may process sign-search history, saved items, posts, replies, reactions, bookmarks, community connections, relationship preferences, trainer information, pledges, service inquiries, and messages or drafts. We may also process a title, signer name, description, or short video when you upload or submit a sign for the community library. Do not include another person’s image, voice, name, or personal story unless you have permission and a lawful basis to share it.

2.4 Device and technical information

Your browser necessarily sends technical information to the hosting server when it requests a page, such as an IP address, browser type, operating system, requested URL, time, and response status. The hosting provider may retain server and security logs to operate the site, prevent abuse, investigate outages, and protect the service. We may also receive coarse diagnostic information when you report a problem.

2.5 Local browser storage

The current pilot saves much of the profile, preference, saved-item, and interaction state in your browser under a user-specific local-storage key. That information is available to the browser profile on the device where you used BRIDGEit. Clearing site data, changing browser profiles, or using another device can remove or make that local state unavailable. Local storage is not a promise that the information is backed up or synchronised to the cloud.

2.6 Information we do not intentionally request

We do not intentionally request passwords, government identification numbers, payment-card numbers, precise health records, or a diagnosis as a condition of using BRIDGEit. Avoid placing those details into free-text fields, uploads, messages, or videos. If you voluntarily submit sensitive information, it may be processed with the content in which you submitted it and may be visible to the audience you selected.

3. How and why we use information

We use information only for purposes connected to operating, securing, improving, and communicating about BRIDGEit. Depending on the circumstances and the law that applies, the relevant basis may be performance of a requested service, your consent, our legitimate interest in running a safe and useful service, or compliance with a legal obligation.

  • Provide the site: load pages, return sign-search results, show community content, remember choices, and deliver features you request.
  • Authenticate accounts: send and verify email one-time codes, maintain sessions, prevent unauthorised access, and respond to account requests.
  • Support communication: show the profile and communication preferences you choose, support connection requests, and help members understand how to contact one another.
  • Moderate and protect people: investigate reports, prevent spam and abuse, enforce the Terms of Use, protect the service, and respond to security incidents.
  • Improve access: understand which features work, fix errors, improve keyboard and screen-reader behaviour, and make content easier to discover. We seek to use aggregated or de-identified information for broad product learning where practical.
  • Communicate with you: send sign-in codes, service notices, responses to support requests, and material changes to the service or these documents.
  • Meet legal duties: keep records, respond to lawful requests, exercise or defend legal rights, and meet applicable tax, safety, or regulatory obligations.

We do not use the contents of private account areas to make decisions about your eligibility for housing, employment, credit, education, healthcare, or insurance. BRIDGEit is not a diagnostic, emergency, medical, legal, or financial service.

4. When information is shared

4.1 With the audience you choose

Information you place in a public, community, member-visible, connection-visible, or other shared feature may be shown to the audience described by that feature. A video or post that you submit to a community library can be copied, screenshotted, or redistributed by people who can view it. Choose visibility carefully and remove content you no longer want to share when the feature allows it.

4.2 Service providers

We use carefully selected providers to host the website and support specific functions. The current implementation uses Supabase for email authentication and community data or media access, a web-hosting provider for the deployed site and server logs, and an email delivery provider configured for one-time codes. Providers may process information only as needed to deliver their service, protect their infrastructure, or comply with law.

4.3 Legal, safety, and business disclosures

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with a valid legal process, protect a person from serious harm, investigate suspected fraud or abuse, enforce our agreements, or protect the rights, property, and security of BRIDGEit, its users, and service providers. If BRIDGEit is reorganised, sold, or combined with another project, information may transfer as part of that transaction subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal requirements.

We do not sell personal information, rent member lists, or share your email address with advertisers for their own marketing. We do not use your information for targeted advertising on the current site.

5. Storage, international processing, and retention

Information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live because our hosting, authentication, storage, and email providers operate internationally. We take reasonable steps to use providers with appropriate security commitments and to limit transfers to what is needed for the service. Local law may give you rights to ask for more information about transfer safeguards.

We keep information for as long as it is needed for the purpose described here, for as long as your account or contribution remains active, or as required for safety, dispute resolution, audit, or legal obligations. Email authentication records are retained according to the authentication provider’s account and security processes. Community content may remain visible while it is published, unless you remove it or we remove it under our moderation process. Device-local data remains until you clear it, the site overwrites it, or your browser removes it.

When information is no longer needed, we aim to delete it, anonymise it, or restrict access. Backups and security logs may take longer to age out, and a limited record may be retained when necessary to prevent repeat abuse or meet a legal obligation.

6. Your choices and privacy rights

You can browse many parts of BRIDGEit without an account. You can choose whether to sign in, what profile fields to complete, which visibility option to use, whether to upload content, and whether to opt into relationship or marketplace features. You may stop using the site at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing, portability, objection, or withdrawal of consent. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. These rights are subject to lawful exceptions, such as information we must keep for security or legal reasons.

To make a request, contact BRIDGEit through the support method published on the website or by replying to a service email. Tell us the email address associated with the account, the request you are making, and any information needed to verify that you control the account. We will not ask you to send an email one-time code to us. We may need to ask for additional information to protect an account from an unauthorised request.

You can clear device-local BRIDGEit data through your browser’s site-data controls. If you ask us to delete an account or community contribution, deletion from one display or cache may not remove copies that other people already downloaded or shared.

7. Security and responsible disclosure

We use HTTPS for the live website, access controls, provider security settings, and limited-purpose authentication tokens. No online service is perfectly secure. Keep your email account secure, do not forward one-time codes, use a private device when possible, and tell us promptly if you believe an account or contribution has been used without permission.

Please do not probe, overload, scrape, or attempt to bypass access controls. If you believe you found a security issue, contact BRIDGEit through the site’s support channel with enough detail for us to reproduce it. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, or unnecessary personal information in a report.

8. Children and vulnerable people

BRIDGEit is not directed to children who cannot lawfully consent to online services. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us so we can review the account and take appropriate action. Community contributors should avoid identifying children or vulnerable people in a video, post, or story unless they have the required permission and a clear, safe purpose.

9. Changes, contact, and interpretation

We may update this policy when the product, providers, law, or safety practices change. We will change the effective date and, when a change is material, use a reasonable in-product or email notice. The current version is always available from the footer of the website.

If a translation conflicts with this English version, the English version controls to the extent permitted by law. Questions, requests, and concerns should be sent through the support method published by BRIDGEit or the service email associated with your account. We will try to acknowledge privacy requests promptly and handle them within the period required by applicable law.

Plain-language summary: share only what you need to share; choose the audience that fits; protect your one-time code; and contact us if you want to review, correct, or remove information connected to your BRIDGEit use.
